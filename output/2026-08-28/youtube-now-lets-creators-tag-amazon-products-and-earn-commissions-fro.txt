YouTube now lets creators tag Amazon products and earn commissions from purchases | TechCrunch

YouTube announced on Thursday that eligible creators in the U.S. can now tag Amazon products in their content and receive a cut of sales. If enabled, YouTube’s systems can automatically review a creator’s recent uploads to identify and tag eligible Amazon products.
