Apple TV is raising its subscription prices again

Now, Apple TV subscriptions will cost $14.99 per month, up from the previous $12.99 per month. Apple One , the bundle that includes subscriptions to services like iCloud+, TV, Music, and Arcade, will now cost $21.95 per month, up from $19.95.
