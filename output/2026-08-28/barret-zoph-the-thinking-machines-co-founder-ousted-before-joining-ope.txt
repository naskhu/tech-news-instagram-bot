Barret Zoph, the Thinking Machines co-founder ousted before joining OpenAI, is now at Google | TechCrunch

Barret Zoph, a co-founder of the AI startup Thinking Machines who left the company earlier this year to rejoin OpenAI, has found yet another job. Zoph spent only five months at OpenAI, where he was tasked with heading AI enterprise sales.
