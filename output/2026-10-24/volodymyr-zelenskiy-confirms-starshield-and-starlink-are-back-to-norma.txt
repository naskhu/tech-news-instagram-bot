Volodymyr Zelenskiy confirms Starshield and Starlink are back to normal.

The Ukrainian president posted on X that the services have been “restored to the speeds we had before the restrictions .” Reuters reports that the defense ministry said the connection remained stable at over 400 kph, crucial for countering Russian drones.
