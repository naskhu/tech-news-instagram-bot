Android rolls out local ‘Documents’ backup that syncs to Google Drive

Google now lets Android users automatically upload files and other downloads from their phone or tablet to Google Drive thanks to a new “Documents” backup. Specifically, a new “Android backups” folder will be added to your My Drive.
