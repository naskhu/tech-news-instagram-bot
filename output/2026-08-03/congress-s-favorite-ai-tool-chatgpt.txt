Congress’s favorite AI tool? ChatGPT

OpenAI received roughly 90% of all spending on AI tools by House offices, committees and institutional accounts during the year ending in March 31, per House disbursement records. Anthropic’s Claude trailed in second place at $13,160 across 37 transactions.
