A security flaw in widely used crime lab equipment exposed digital DNA evidence to undetectable tampering.

Using Antrophic’s Claude to exploit a security flaw that’s existed for decades, researchers successfully added and removed DNA profiles from files, meaning bad actors could theoretically frame an innocent person or erase a suspect.
