Uber partners with Zipline on Eats drone deliveries

The companies are aiming for 1 million daily drone deliveries by 2029. Uber is teaming up with drone company Zipline to start airborne takeout deliveries later this year , with the goal of reaching one million daily drone deliveries by 2029.
