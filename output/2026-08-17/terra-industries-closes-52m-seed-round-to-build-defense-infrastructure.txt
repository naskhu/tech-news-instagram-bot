Terra Industries closes $52M seed round to build defense infrastructure for the Global South

African defense tech company Terra Industries on Monday announced an additional $18 million in funding, bringing its seed round to $52 million. Earlier this year, Terra announced back-to-back funding rounds of $11.75 million and $22 million, respectively.
