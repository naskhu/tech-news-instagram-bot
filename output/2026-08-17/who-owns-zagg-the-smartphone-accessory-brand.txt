Who Owns Zagg, The Smartphone Accessory Brand?

In early 2021, Zagg was officially bought by a group led by Evercel, Inc. The brand still pushes out new InvisibleShield protectors, mophie chargers (which are among the best portable chargers ), and Gear4 cases alongside every major smartphone launch.
