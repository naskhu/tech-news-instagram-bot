Did Unitree’s ‘Superman’ robot just run faster than Usain Bolt?

The just-IPO’d Chinese robot maker claims it beat two human world records in one video: highest standing high jump and fastest running speed. Bolt’s top speed was 12.22 meters per second; Unitree claims 12.66.
