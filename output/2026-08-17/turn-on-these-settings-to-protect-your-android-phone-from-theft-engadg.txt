Turn on these settings to protect your Android phone from theft - Engadget

For Samsung users, the order is typically Settings → Security and Privacy → Lost Device Protection → Theft Protection. Enable Theft Detection Lock and Offline Device Lock so your phone auto-locks and remains that way, even if the thief yanks it offline.
