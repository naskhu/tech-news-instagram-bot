Nvidia discloses $21B stake in SpaceX

Elon Musk’s rocket conglomerate’s shares have fallen sharply since its June initial public offering, meaning Nvidia’s stake would now be worth $17 billion. It has also invested in Cursor, the code-editing start-up that SpaceX acquired for $60 billion this week.
