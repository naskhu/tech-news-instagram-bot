Meet the only known trebuchet casualty in history

During a medieval siege, a trebuchet scored a chance hit one unlucky defender. The man’s skeleton lay beneath the chapel of Scotland’s Stirling Castle, along with the remains of several other people who had clearly died violent deaths.
