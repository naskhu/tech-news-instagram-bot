Transportation secretary blames ‘routine’ equipment upgrade for grounding flights nationwide | TechCrunch

Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said Saturday that a nationwide ground stop halting flights across the United States was caused by seemingly routine maintenance work. This was only the third nationwide ground stop of the 21st century.
