New York alleges TikTok gave teens, children a placebo safety feature instead of a real one | TechCrunch

Reuters reports that TikTok’s experiments involved thousands of users, including teens and children, who had opted to use safety features like “Algo Refresh,” a tool that allows users to reset their recommendations and stop seeing unwanted or potentially harmful content.
