This is the world's most advanced robotic servicing satellite—that we know about

China launched a satellite with a robotic arm into geosynchronous orbit in 2016. The public-private partnership brought Northrop Grumman’s preexisting commercial satellite servicing program together with DARPA’s Robotic Servicing of Geosynchronous Satellites (RSGS) program.
