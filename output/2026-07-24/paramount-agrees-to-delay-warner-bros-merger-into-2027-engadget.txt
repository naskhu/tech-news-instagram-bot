Paramount agrees to delay Warner Bros. merger into 2027 - Engadget

Paramount Skydance has agreed to pause its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery until June 2027, while a judge considers a recently filed joint lawsuit from 12 states to block the deal, The New York Times reports.
