SAP’s cloud is still booming, and that was enough to calm the AI-doom fears

Europe’s biggest software company said cloud revenue grew 22% to €6.28 billion in the second quarter, beating forecasts, SAP announced . A strong cloud quarter is the clearest answer SAP can give: its customers are still signing up, not walking away.
