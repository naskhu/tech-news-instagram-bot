OnTrac notifies customers of data breach after network hack

To help exposed customers mitigate the risks that may arise from the exposure of their sensitive data, OnTrac is offering free-of-charge access to a 12-month credit monitoring and identity protection service via CyberScout, with a 90-day enrollment deadline.
