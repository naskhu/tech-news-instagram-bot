AMD teams up with Eric Trump-backed Foundation to build humanoid robots

Under the deal, announced on July 23 , Foundation’s robots will run on AMD’s Ryzen AI Embedded X100 Series processors, the embedded chips the company introduced in January. Neither company disclosed how many chips the deal covers or what it is worth.
