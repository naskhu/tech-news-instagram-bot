Judge Moves to Dismiss Suit That Alleges WhatsApp's E2E Encryption Is a Sham

A judge has dismissed a lawsuit that alleged WhatsApp can secretly bypass end-to-end encryption to view users' messages due to a lack of evidence. The lawsuit, filed in January, originally claimed that unnamed whistleblowers had uncovered WhatsApp’s ability to circumvent the encryption.
