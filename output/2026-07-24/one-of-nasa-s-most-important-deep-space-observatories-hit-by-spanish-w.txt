One of NASA’s Most Important Deep Space Observatories Hit by Spanish Wildfires

On Friday, wildfires in Spain overran one of three observatories the US National Aeronautics and Space Administration uses to connect with spacecraft, including Voyager 1 , the James Webb Space Telescope, and the recently completed Artemis mission around the Moon.
