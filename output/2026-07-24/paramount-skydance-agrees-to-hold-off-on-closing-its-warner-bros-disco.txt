Paramount Skydance agrees to hold off on closing its Warner Bros. Discovery merger for up to a year.

Meanwhile, the “ticking fee” Paramount will owe WBD shareholders for any delay starts counting in October, and could add up to $1.7 billion if the delay lasts until next June.
