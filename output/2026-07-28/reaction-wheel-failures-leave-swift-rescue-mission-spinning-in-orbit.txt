Reaction wheel failures leave Swift rescue mission spinning in orbit

Extending Swift’s scientific mission was not the only consideration when NASA officials selected Katalyst to build and launch a satellite to boost Swift’s orbit. Katalyst’s Link spacecraft is still generating sufficient electrical power in communication with ground teams, NASA said.
