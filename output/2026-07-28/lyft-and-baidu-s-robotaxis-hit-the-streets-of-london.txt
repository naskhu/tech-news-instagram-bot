Lyft and Baidu’s robotaxis hit the streets of London.

After the UK opened the door for autonomous ridehail vehicles , several companies have indicated they intend to launch in London, including Uber, Wayve, and Waymo. Lyft and Baidu’s vehicles will stick to the neighborhood of Brent for their initial testing.
