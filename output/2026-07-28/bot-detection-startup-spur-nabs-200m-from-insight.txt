Bot-detection startup Spur nabs $200M from Insight

Spur Intelligence, a cybersecurity startup based in Lake Mary, Florida, has raised a $200 million round led by Insight Partners. Detecting malicious traffic has, of course, been a hill corporate security teams have been climbing for eons.
