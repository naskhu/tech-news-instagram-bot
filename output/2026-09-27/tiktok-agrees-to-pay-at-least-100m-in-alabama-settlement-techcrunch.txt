TikTok agrees to pay at least $100M in Alabama settlement | TechCrunch

According to the Alabama attorney general’s office , TikTok could end up paying the state a total of $300 million, depending on “certain conditions.” The company also agreed to introduce a two-hour daily time limit for underage users, with additional parental controls…
