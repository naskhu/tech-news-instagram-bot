John Goodman joins the cast of The Last of Us.

Ian Alexander, who voiced Lev in The Last of Us: Part II , will play opposite him as another original character, Paco. Laura Bailey, who voiced Abby in the game, will join the cast as Elizabeth, a leader of the Seraphites cult.
