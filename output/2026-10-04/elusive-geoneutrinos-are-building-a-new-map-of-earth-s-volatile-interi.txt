Elusive ‘Geoneutrinos’ Are Building a New Map of Earth’s Volatile Interior

Geoneutrinos are produced in processes that heat the interior of the planet. What makes the detections at SNO+ special is the experiment’s location: These are the first geoneutrinos measured in the western hemisphere, offering a new perspective on Earth’s radioactive interior.
