Google takes Dreambeans to India in bid to bring AI-generated personal stories to the masses | TechCrunch

Introduced in June, Google’s Dreambeans uses information from connected Google services, including Calendar, Gemini, Gmail, Photos, Search, and YouTube, to proactively generate personalized, illustrated stories about users’ interests and everyday lives, without requiring them to enter a prompt.
