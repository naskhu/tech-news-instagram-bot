Instagram puts new limits on undisclosed AI profiles | TechCrunch

Instagram says people who use AI to edit photos, polish captions, create graphics, or make other creative tweaks don’t need to use the AI-generated profile label. Users objected to having their public Instagram content used without an explicit opt-in.
