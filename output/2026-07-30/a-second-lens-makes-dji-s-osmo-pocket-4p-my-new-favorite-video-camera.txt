A second lens makes DJI’s Osmo Pocket 4P my new favorite video camera

DJI’s Osmo Pocket 4P is a pretty simple proposition: an Osmo Pocket 4 with a second camera lens slapped inside. DJI says the Pocket 4P will last for 210 minutes of use, half an hour less than the Pocket 4.
