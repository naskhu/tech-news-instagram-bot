AI price wars: OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna prices by 80% as model competition shifts toward cost

OpenAI says Luna will now cost $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens, for a combined input-plus-output price of $1.40 per million tokens. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite costs $0.30 per million input tokens and $2.50 per million output tokens.
