Apple reports Q3 2026 earnings: $109.4 billion in revenue, up 16% - 9to5Mac

The company reported $109.4 billion in revenue, $29.8 billion in net profit, and $2.02 in earnings per share. Last year, for its third fiscal quarter, Apple reported $94.04 billion in revenue, $23.43 billion in net profit, and earnings per share of $1.57.
