Apple spent a record $11.73 billion in R&D during Q3 2026

In its quarterly results, Apple reported that research and development expenses climbed to a record $11.73 billion, continuing the sharp acceleration in spending seen throughout fiscal 2026. Last quarter, Apple reported research and development expenses of $11.4 billion, a 34% year-over-year jump.
