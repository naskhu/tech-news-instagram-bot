South Korea fines telco giant KT $39 million for customer data breach

South Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) has fined telecommunications giant KT Corporation KRW 53.979 billion ($39 million) over data protection violations. PIPC launched an investigation into a potential data breach on September 10, 2025, following user reports of fraudulent micropayments.
