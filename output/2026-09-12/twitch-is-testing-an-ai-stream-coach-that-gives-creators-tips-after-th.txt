Twitch is testing an AI ‘Stream Coach’ that gives creators tips after their streams.

The AI coaching feature is only available to a small group of streamers right now. Unlike Twitch’s generative AI training toggle , which is opt out, the company says this feature would be opt-in if it’s ever expanded beyond this test.
