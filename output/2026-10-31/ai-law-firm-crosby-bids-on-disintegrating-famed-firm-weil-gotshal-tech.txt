AI law firm Crosby bids on disintegrating, famed firm Weil Gotshal | TechCrunch

Two-year old AI-first law firm Crosby has put in a bid to buy some of the assets of Weil Gotshal, including its trademark, its brand, and some unspecified internal data, TechCrunch has confirmed.
