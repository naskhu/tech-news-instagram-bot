Science Corporation implants its vision-restoring chip in its first Stargardt disease patient | TechCrunch

Science Corporation CEO Max Hodak announced that the company has implanted its vision-restoring chip , PRIMA, into its first patient with Stargardt disease, an inherited eye disease that gradually destroys central vision.
