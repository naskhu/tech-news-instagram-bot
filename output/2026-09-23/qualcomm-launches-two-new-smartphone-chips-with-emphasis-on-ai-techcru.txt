Qualcomm launches two new smartphone chips with emphasis on AI | TechCrunch

Qualcomm today announced two flagship smartphone processors, called Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 and Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, with a focus on facilitating better AI-focused features. Plus, it has Qualcomm’s new voice bubble tech, which isolates users’ noise during calls.
