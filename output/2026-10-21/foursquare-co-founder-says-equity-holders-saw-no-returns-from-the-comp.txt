Foursquare co-founder says equity holders saw no returns from the company's acquisition | TechCrunch

Last month, advertising company Infillion acquired location-data platform Foursquare, a once popular social network, at an undisclosed value. Now, in a new blog post , Foursquare co-founder and former CEO Dennis Crowley said that investors didn’t see any returns from the sale.
