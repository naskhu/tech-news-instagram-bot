Insecure TLS certificates will be ‘a massive cleanup job’ for system admins.

Cryptography engineer Filippo Valsorda told Ars that the number of affected devices means it’ll take longer to clean up than Heartbleed , an infamous OpenSSL vulnerability that rocked the internet in 2014 and took years to fix.
