Streaming guide JustWatch becomes a streamer itself | TechCrunch

The media and entertainment company behind the streaming guide, the JustWatch Group, initially announced its plans to venture into streaming last month. Like most low-cost services, JustWatch’s content lineup focuses on older movies and shows, as well as more indie content.
