Researchers devise a full-color night vision goggle

Human eyes don’t register the infrared portion of the light spectrum because infrared photons don’t carry enough energy to trigger the signaling pathway inside our light-sensing cells. Infrared illumination through the device indeed triggered photocurrents in those cells.
