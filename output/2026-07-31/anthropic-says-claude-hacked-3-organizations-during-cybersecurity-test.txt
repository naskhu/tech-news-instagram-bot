Anthropic Says Claude Hacked 3 Organizations During Cybersecurity Tests

Anthropic disclosed on Thursday that its AI models gained unauthorized access to the systems of three different unnamed organizations during cybersecurity testing. Anthropic said that the incidents involved Opus 4.7, Mythos 5 , and an internal research test model.
