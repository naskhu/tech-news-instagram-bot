OpenAI says its new GPT 5.6 models are becoming more cost-efficient

As per the updated pricing, GPT-5.6 Luna now costs $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 per million output tokens, down from $1 and $6. In all other use cases, you really don't need GPT-5.6 Sol Fast mode.
