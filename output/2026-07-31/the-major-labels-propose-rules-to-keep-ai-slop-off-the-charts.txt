The major labels propose rules to keep AI slop off the charts

Several record labels, including the big three — Universal Music Group, Sony Music, and Warner Music Group — have proposed rules regarding chart eligibility for AI songs . That would create a set of standardized labels for AI-generated and AI-assisted music.
