India is starting to pay for apps, not just download them | TechCrunch

India’s app revenue saw its fastest growth in Q2 among major app markets, generating more than $200 million in quarterly consumer spending, per Sensor Tower’s data shared with TechCrunch. Much of India’s app revenue growth is also being driven by non-gaming apps.
