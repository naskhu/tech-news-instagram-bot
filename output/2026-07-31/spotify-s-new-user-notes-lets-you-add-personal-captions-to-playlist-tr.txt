Spotify’s new ‘User Notes’ lets you add personal captions to playlist tracks - 9to5Mac

Spotify has announced User Notes (via TechCrunch ), a new feature that lets users append short, 240-character-long notes to individual tracks on their playlists. Spotify’s new “User Notes” feature is available for playlists users created themselves or have contributed to.
