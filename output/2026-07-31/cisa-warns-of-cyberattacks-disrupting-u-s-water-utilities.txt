CISA warns of cyberattacks disrupting U.S. water utilities

Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) is warning of a significant increase in attacks targeting internet-exposed programmable logic controllers (PLCs) in the water and wastewater systems sector. CISA recommends immediately removing these assets from direct internet exposure.
