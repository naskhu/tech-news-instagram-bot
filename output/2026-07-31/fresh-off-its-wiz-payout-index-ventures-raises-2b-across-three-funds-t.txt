Fresh off its Wiz payout, Index Ventures raises $2B across three funds | TechCrunch

Index Ventures has raised $2 billion in fresh capital across three funds, the firm announced on Friday. The fresh capital haul comes two years after Index raised $2.3 billion across two funds , including $800 million toward a predecessor venture fund.
