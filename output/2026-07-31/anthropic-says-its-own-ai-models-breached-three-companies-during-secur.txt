Anthropic says its own AI models breached three companies during security tests | TechCrunch

Anthropic said the July 21 OpenAI incident prompted the company to conduct its own cybersecurity evaluation. Anthropic said the incidents involved three different Claude models: Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an internal research test model.
