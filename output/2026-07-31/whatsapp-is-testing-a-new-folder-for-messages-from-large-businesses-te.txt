WhatsApp is testing a new folder for messages from large businesses | TechCrunch

At the moment, small businesses and individual accounts using WhatsApp Business are exempt from this feature. WhatsApp said it could explore moving business messages from small businesses to the new “Offers & Updates” folder in the future.
