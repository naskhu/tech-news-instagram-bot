ChatGPT now lets users create iMessage and WhatsApp stickers - 9to5Mac

Users can turn any photo into an iMessage or WhatsApp sticker, or use images as inspiration to create custom packs. Once the sticker is done, users can tap a “Add to chat apps” button, which lets them export to supported chat apps.
