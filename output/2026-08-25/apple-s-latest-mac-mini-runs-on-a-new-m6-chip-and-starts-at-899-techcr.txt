Apple's latest Mac Mini runs on a new M6 chip, and starts at $899 | TechCrunch

The new processor has a 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, as compared to the 10-core CPU and 10-core GPU on the M4. The M5 Max base model comes with 36GB of unified memory and 512GB of storage.
