Inaudible sounds used to fingerprint browsers catch AliExpress red handed

Chinese retailer AliExpress has been caught fingerprinting visitors after one of the metrics—an outdated technique that measures inaudible sounds it sends to browsers—impeded a researcher’s ability to use his bluetooth headphones. Each time he loaded AliExpress, the phone audio stopped.
