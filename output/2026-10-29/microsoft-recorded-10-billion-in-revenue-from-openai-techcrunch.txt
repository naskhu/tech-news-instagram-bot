Microsoft recorded $10 billion in revenue from OpenAI | TechCrunch

Microsoft generated $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI, including revenue-sharing payments, in the quarter ended September 30, the company said in a regulatory filing on Wednesday. That money represents amounts OpenAI owes for previous transactions, not additional revenue.
