Apple launches its new touchscreen MacBook Pro | TechCrunch

The new MacBook Pro more fully embraces the touchscreen, offering a new PROLED XDR Display in both 14-inch and 16-inch screen sizes. The Touchscreen MacBook Pro 14-inch starts at $2,899, and the 16-inch version starts at $3,399.
