Microsoft just shared Azure's actual revenue in dollars: $33.2 billion | TechCrunch

Microsoft has never disclosed Azure’s quarterly revenue in dollars. The disclosure lets investors line up Azure’s performance more directly against Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud, whose parent companies already report cloud revenue in dollars every quarter.
