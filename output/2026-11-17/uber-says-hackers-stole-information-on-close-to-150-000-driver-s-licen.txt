Uber says hackers stole information on close to 150,000 driver's licenses during data breach | TechCrunch

Uber is notifying close to 150,000 drivers that their personal and driver’s license information was stolen during an October data breach at one of the company’s tech providers. This is Uber’s second known data breach this year.
