Cybersecurity AI startup Corma reaches $1B valuation in about 3 months | TechCrunch

Three months after introducing its product, AI cybersecurity startup Corma has raised a $125 million Series A round at a $1 billion valuation led by NEA, the company said. Corma offers AI models and agents trained for cybersecurity defense.
