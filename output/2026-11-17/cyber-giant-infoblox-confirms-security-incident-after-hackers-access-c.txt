Cyber giant Infoblox confirms security incident after hackers access 'company information' | TechCrunch

Cybersecurity and automation giant Infoblox has confirmed a cybersecurity incident, during which hackers accessed company information through one of its technology vendors. One of the people said sensitive customer data was taken in the breach.
