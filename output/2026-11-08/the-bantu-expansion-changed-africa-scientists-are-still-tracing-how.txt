The Bantu expansion changed Africa. Scientists are still tracing how.

In the 2024 genetic analysis, Schlebusch and her colleagues found that some Bantu-speaking groups in central Africa share about an eighth of their DNA with rainforest hunter-gatherers, and some southern African Bantu speakers share a similar amount with Khoisan groups.
