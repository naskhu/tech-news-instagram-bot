Muse can now place calls to US businesses — if you pay | TechCrunch

After initially testing the feature in September, Meta’s consumer AI agent Muse is now able to place calls to U.S. businesses on behalf of its users. Currently, Muse subscription plans start at $20 per month for 500 million Muse tokens per week.
