Apple bucks downward trend as reports show global smartphone shipment and sales declines

Four Counterpoint Research reports published this week reveal drops in smartphone sales and shipments in India, Latin America, Europe, and China. Even so, Apple gained nine percentage points, matching Samsung at 34% of the shipments share each.
