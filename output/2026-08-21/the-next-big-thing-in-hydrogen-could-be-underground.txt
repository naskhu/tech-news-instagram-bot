The next big thing in hydrogen could be underground

Today, hydrogen is overwhelmingly made using fossil fuels, generally natural gas. HyTerra, an Australian company, is searching for hydrogen across Nebraska and Kansas, and it’s already found samples of gas with hydrogen concentrations up to 96%.
