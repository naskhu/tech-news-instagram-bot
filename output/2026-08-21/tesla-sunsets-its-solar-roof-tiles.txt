Tesla sunsets its Solar Roof tiles

The dedicated Solar Roof page ( tesla.com/solarroof ) that launched almost a decade ago now automatically redirects to Tesla’s solar panel page instead, and the “Solar Roof” option is no longer available under the navigation menu for Tesla Energy products.
