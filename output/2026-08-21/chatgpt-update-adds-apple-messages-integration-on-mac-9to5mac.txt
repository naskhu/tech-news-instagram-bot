ChatGPT update adds Apple Messages integration on Mac - 9to5Mac

ChatGPT’s latest update adds a new Mac integration: the ability to work with conversations from Apple’s Messages app. The new Apple Messages plugin lets ChatGPT read and search chats on a Mac, and prepare and send messages.
