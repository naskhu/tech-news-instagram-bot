Taylor Farms recalls jalapeño products for Salmonella amid Cyclospora outbreak

Amid a massive Cyclospora outbreak linked to its iceberg lettuce, Taylor Farms is now recalling products containing jalapeños, which were linked last week to a large, multi-state Salmonella outbreak. We will update this story if Taylor Farms responds.
