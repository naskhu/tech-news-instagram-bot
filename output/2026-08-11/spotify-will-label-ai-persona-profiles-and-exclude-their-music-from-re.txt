Spotify will label 'AI Persona' profiles and exclude their music from recommendations | TechCrunch

Spotify will begin labeling AI-generated artists with “AI Persona” profile tags and ban their music from its editorial and algorithmic recommendations, the company announced on Tuesday. The labels themselves will appear starting next month.
