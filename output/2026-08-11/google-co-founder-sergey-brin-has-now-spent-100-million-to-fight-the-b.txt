Google co-founder Sergey Brin has now spent $100 million to fight the billionaire tax

Google co-founder Sergey Brin has donated another $20 million to Build a Better California, an organization advocating against California’s proposed billionaire tax, according to a new filing . The windfall from this proposed tax would mostly go toward funding California’s healthcare programs.
