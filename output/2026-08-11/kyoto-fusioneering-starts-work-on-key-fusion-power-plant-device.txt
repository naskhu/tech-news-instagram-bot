Kyoto Fusioneering starts work on key fusion power plant device

Breeding blankets harvest energy from the fusion reactor while also generating fresh fusion fuel. Kyoto Fusioneering is also developing systems to heat fusion fuel into a plasma, recycle unburned fuel from the exhaust, and harvest heat to generate electricity.
