AMD will acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2 billion | TechCrunch

World Labs justified the deal in a statement saying that AI development required “close collaboration across model research, systems and compute.” AMD, in turn, says that understanding frontier workloads, like those created at World Labs, will shape its chip-making roadmap.
