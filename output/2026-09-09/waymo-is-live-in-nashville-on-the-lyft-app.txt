Waymo is live in Nashville on the Lyft app.

Lyft customers hailing a ride in Waymo’s service area (which includes Downtown/Broadway, North Nashville/Germantown, East Nashville, Midtown, and South Nashville) could be matched with a robotaxi. Customers who would prefer a robotaxi ride can also book one through Waymo’s own app.
