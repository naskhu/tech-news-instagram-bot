A hacker stole $340M in a crypto heist, then returned most of it | TechCrunch

Liquid Network, launched in 2018 by crypto firm Blockstream, said in a post on X on Sunday that a “white hat” hacker stole the funds from the network’s wallet, and that it has paused operations until the incident is resolved.
