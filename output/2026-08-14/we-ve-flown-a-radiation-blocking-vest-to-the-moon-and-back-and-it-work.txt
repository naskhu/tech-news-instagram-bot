We've flown a radiation-blocking vest to the Moon and back, and it worked

To test this idea, StemRad’s team flew a wearable radiation-shielding vest called AstroRad to the Moon and back aboard NASA’s uncrewed Artemis I mission, then used the flight data to calculate how it would perform during an actual solar storm.
