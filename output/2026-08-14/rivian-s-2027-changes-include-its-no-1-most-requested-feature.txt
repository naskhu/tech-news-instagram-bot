Rivian's 2027 changes include its No. 1 most-requested feature

The Performance R1s feature Rivian’s tri-motor powertrain, still with 850 hp (634 kW) and a range of 371 miles (597 km). There are still three interior trim options with the new names: Black Mountain, Ocean Coast, and Slate Sky.
