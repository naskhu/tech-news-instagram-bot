Uber and Pony.ai plan to bring 2,000 robotaxis to Europe

Chinese autonomous vehicle maker Pony AI and Uber plan to bring 2,000 robotaxis to four cities in Europe as part of an expanded partnership. Uber has partnered with more than 30 autonomous vehicle companies , including Pony.ai, over the past several years.
