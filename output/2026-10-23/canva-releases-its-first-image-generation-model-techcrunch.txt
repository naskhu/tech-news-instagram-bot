Canva releases its first image generation model | TechCrunch

Design platform Canva released its first image generation model, called Canva Craft, developed by its research team to compete with same-category models from Google, OpenAI, ByteDance, and Meta. In 2024, Canva acquired media generation platform Leonardo.ai .
