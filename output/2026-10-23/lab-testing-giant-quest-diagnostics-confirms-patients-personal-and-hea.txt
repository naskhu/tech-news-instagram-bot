Lab testing giant Quest Diagnostics confirms patients' personal and health data was compromised during breach | TechCrunch

Medical lab testing giant Quest Diagnostics has confirmed a cyberattack and data breach affecting an as-yet unspecified amount of sensitive patient information stored in its systems, according to a legally required disclosure with U.S. regulators.
