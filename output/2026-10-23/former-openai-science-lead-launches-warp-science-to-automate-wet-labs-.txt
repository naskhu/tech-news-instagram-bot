Former OpenAI science lead launches Warp Science to automate wet labs with robots | TechCrunch

Former OpenAI science lead Kevin Weil has launched Warp Sciences, a new AI startup focused on automating lab work for drug discovery. By automating wet-lab work, Warp Sciences aims to accelerate drug discovery using robots that can operate around the clock.
