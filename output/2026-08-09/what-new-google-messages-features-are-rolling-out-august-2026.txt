What new Google Messages features are rolling out [August 2026]

Like other Google apps, Messages for Android A/B tests many features. Google Messages is letting you more extensively customize conversations with “Chat theme.” Replacing “Change colors” from 2024, you can access this new option from the three-dot overflow menu.
