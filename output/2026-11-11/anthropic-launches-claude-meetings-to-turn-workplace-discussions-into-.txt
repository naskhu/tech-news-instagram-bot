Anthropic launches Claude Meetings to turn workplace discussions into plans and tasks | TechCrunch

Anthropic announced new workplace features on Tuesday, including Claude Meetings, which brings Claude into video calls to begin working on tasks while participants are still talking. The company also unveiled Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets.
