Oura to acquire AI-native primary care startup Counsel Health for $625M | TechCrunch

Oura announced on Tuesday that it’s acquiring AI-native primary care company Counsel Health for $625 million. Users can connect with Counsel’s medical AI to get personalized medical advice, and chat with a physician when needed, all within the Oura app.
