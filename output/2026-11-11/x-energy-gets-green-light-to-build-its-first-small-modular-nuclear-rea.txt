X-energy gets green light to build its first small modular nuclear reactors | TechCrunch

Nuclear startup X-energy announced Monday that it had been given the go-ahead from federal officials to build its first four commercial reactors. X-energy says the approval is the first for an advanced nuclear reactor at an industrial site in the United States.
