OpenAI acquires Odessia, travel startup created by Sonder founder | TechCrunch

OpenAI has acquired an AI travel agent called Odessia, according to Odessia’s founder, Francis Davidson. Davidson is well known in the travel industry after he founded a travel startup called Sonder as a college student in 2014.
