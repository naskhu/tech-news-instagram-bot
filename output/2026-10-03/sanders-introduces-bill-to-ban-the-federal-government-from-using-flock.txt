Sanders introduces bill to ban the federal government from using Flock | TechCrunch

Sanders (D-Vermont) on Friday introduced the Ban Flock Act , which would bar federal agencies from using automatic license plate readers (ALPRs) or tapping into data collected by readers run by local police and private companies.
