Four AI agents coordinating in real time outperformed Claude Opus 4.8 on enterprise coding tasks

On a benchmark of long-horizon questions over production repositories, a team of agents powered by AgentRadio nearly doubled task accuracy for four Claude Code agents working independently. The create_thread primitive opens a conversation between participating agents.
