You may soon be able to create and export custom WhatsApp stickers straight from ChatGPT

OpenAI is working on a feature that allows users to export custom stickers created on ChatGPT straight into WhatsApp. Users can tap the “Add to WhatsApp” option to add their created stickers to WhatsApp as a custom sticker pack.
