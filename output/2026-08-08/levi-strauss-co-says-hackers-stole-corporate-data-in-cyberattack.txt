Levi Strauss & Co. says hackers stole corporate data in cyberattack

Levi Strauss & Co. (Levi’s) says that hackers used social engineering on three of its employees to gain access to and steal corporate data stored on their machines. The company has disclosed the incident in a filing with the U.S.
