Judge rules Meta caused "public nuisance" and must fund mental health treatment

A New Mexico judge yesterday ordered Meta to pay $567 million for a fund that would alleviate the “public nuisance” created by its social media services. New Mexico Attorney General Raul Torrez sued Meta in a Santa Fe County court in 2023.
