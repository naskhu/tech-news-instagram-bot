Stanford is running 37,000 AI agents as a virtual biotech — and one of its drug designs got independently confirmed by Merck

The resulting system, dubbed the Virtual Biotech , comprises tens of thousands of specialized AI agents overseen by a Chief Scientific Officer (CSO) agent. The agents completed this design autonomously, relying exclusively on data published prior to January 2025.
