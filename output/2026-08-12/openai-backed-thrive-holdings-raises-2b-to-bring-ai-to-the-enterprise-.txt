OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings raises $2B to bring AI to the enterprise | TechCrunch

Thrive Holdings has raised $2 billion in new funding at a $12 billion valuation from investors like SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, and Altimeter Capital. In December 2025, OpenAI took an ownership stake in Thrive Holdings.
