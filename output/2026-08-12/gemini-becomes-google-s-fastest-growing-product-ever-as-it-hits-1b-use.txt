Gemini becomes Google's fastest-growing product ever as it hits 1B users

CEO Sundar Pichai announced today that Gemini has reached 1 billion monthly active users (MAUs), an important milestone for any Google product. Last month, Google said it was still working on Gemini 3.5 Pro and had already started training Gemini 4.
