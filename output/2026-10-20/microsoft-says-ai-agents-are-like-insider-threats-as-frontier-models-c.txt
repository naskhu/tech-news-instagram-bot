Microsoft says AI agents are like insider threats as 'frontier models can't be trusted' | TechCrunch

Microsoft on Monday introduced new security features designed to protect enterprises from the growing threat of AI agents. The self-serving side is that Microsoft wants enterprises to buy agent security products from it, the cloud provider, rather than from Anthropic and OpenAI.
