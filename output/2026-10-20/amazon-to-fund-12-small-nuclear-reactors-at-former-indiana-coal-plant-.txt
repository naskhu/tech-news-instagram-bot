Amazon to fund 12 small nuclear reactors at former Indiana coal plant | TechCrunch

Utility company American Electric Power (AEP) would operate the plant. The new power plant would consist of 12 Xe-100 small modular reactors and be located on the site of a demolished coal power plant just south of Terre Haute.
