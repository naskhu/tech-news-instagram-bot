India ends free ride for larger transactions on its ubiquitous digital payments network | TechCrunch

The country’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) will impose a 0.4% merchant fee on certain payments above ₹2,000 (about $21) from October 15, the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), which operates the network, said on Tuesday.
