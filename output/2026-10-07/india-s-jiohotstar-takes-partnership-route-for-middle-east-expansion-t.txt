India's JioHotstar takes partnership route for Middle East expansion | TechCrunch

JioHotstar , the streaming platform with more than 500 million monthly active users backed by Indian conglomerate Reliance Industries, is expanding into the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) through Starzplay, one of the region’s largest streaming services.
