AI computing startup Lambda to raise $4B ahead of planned IPO | TechCrunch

Cloud provider Lambda is raising up to $4 billion at a $14.5 billion pre-money valuation, marking what could be its last private round before a planned 2027 IPO, according to The Wall Street Journal.
