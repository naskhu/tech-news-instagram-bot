Oracle sends force majeure notice on its New Mexico Stargate data center | TechCrunch

Oracle has sent a force majeure notice to the developer of Project Jupiter, a Stargate data center campus in New Mexico, Bloomberg first reported Thursday. Oracle is not seeking to exit as the campus’s main tenant, according to Bloomberg’s sources.
