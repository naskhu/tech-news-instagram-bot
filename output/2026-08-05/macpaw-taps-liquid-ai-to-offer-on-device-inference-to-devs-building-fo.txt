MacPaw taps Liquid AI to offer on-device inference to devs building for its app store | TechCrunch

Ukraine-based app developer MacPaw has partnered up with Liquid AI to power its products with locally hosted AI models, and eventually make the tech stack available to developers. Notably, Apple already provides its own local models to developers.
