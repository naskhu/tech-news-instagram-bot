Google is bringing Gemini in Classroom to K-12 students.

Students will be able to use Gemini to create things like flashcards or practice quizzes and sync info to Gemini Notebook ( formerly NotebookLM ). Gemini in Classroom initially launched for educators , then to students 18 or older .
