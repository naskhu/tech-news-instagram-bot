Recursive launches Flint, a laptop coding model it says was largely built by AI | TechCrunch

Recursive’s automated research system, which it calls the Eureka Machine, helped with nearly every step of Flint’s development, the company says. Recursive says each new model should make the Eureka Machine a better researcher, helping it build the next one faster.
