Uber puts all 30,000 employees on ChatGPT Work | TechCrunch

Uber originally piloted ChatGPT Work with 1,000 employees earlier this year but has now scaled up its usage to the entire company. Uber says OpenAI worked closely with the company on integrating the chatbot.
