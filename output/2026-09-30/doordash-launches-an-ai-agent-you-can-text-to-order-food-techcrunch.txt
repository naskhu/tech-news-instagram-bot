DoorDash launches an AI agent you can text to order food | TechCrunch

DoorDash announced on Wednesday that it’s launching a text-to-order AI agent that lets users place orders through Apple Messages. When ordering for a group, DoorDash says the agent can handle mixed dietary preferences and different quantities in one order.
