FedEx orders 2,000 electric trucks from Harbinger in $300M deal | TechCrunch

Harbinger, which already has trucks in production, plans to deliver all 2,000 by the end of next year, the company told TechCrunch. FedEx led a $160 million Series C funding round for Harbinger late last year.
