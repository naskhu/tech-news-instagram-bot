Archer buys former rival Wisk Aero

Three years after settling a trade secret lawsuit, electric aircraft company Archer Aviation now owns its former rival, Wisk Aero . Under the terms of the settlement, Archer agreed to make Wisk the exclusive provider of autonomous technology.
