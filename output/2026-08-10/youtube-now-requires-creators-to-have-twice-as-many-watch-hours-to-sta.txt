YouTube now requires creators to have twice as many watch hours to start earning money

The Google-owned company says the update won’t impact creators already in the YouTube Partner Program. Additionally, YouTube announced that creators will need to maintain 10 million Shorts views over a 90-day period to earn money through the Shorts Creators Pool.
