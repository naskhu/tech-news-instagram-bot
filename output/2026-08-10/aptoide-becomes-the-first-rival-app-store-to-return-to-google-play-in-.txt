Aptoide becomes the first rival app store to return to Google Play in the US

As of June 22, 2026, Google began allowing third-party app stores to access Google Play’s app catalog through the Play Catalog Access Program . Epic Games first sued Google in 2020, alleging anticompetitive abuses in the Android app ecosystem.
