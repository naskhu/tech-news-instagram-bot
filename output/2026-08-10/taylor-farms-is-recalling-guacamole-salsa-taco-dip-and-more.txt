Taylor Farms is recalling guacamole, salsa, taco dip, and more.

After health officials linked its lettuce to the nationwide Cyclospora outbreak , Taylor Farms is now recalling over a dozen products containing jalapeños across Walmart, Target, Whole Foods, Trader Joe’s, and Kroger.
