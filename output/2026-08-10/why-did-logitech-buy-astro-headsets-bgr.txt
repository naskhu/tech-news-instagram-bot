Why Did Logitech Buy Astro Headsets? - BGR

In 2017, Logitech made a move, acquiring Astro in a deal worth $85 million. In the mid-2010s, Astro was positioned as a specialist console gaming headset brand with strong links to esports, while today it operates within Logitech's broader gaming ecosystem.
