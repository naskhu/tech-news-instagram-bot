Meta rolls out new AI tools to detect ads that secretly lead to child sexual abuse material | TechCrunch

Meta said its latest AI tools are aimed at people using ads that look harmless to send users to illegal content outside Meta’s platforms. Meta is also using additional AI-driven scans to find child exploitation content that earlier systems may have missed.
