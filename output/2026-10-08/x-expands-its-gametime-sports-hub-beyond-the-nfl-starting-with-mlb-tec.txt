X expands its 'Gametime' sports hub beyond the NFL, starting with MLB | TechCrunch

At Advertising Week New York, X said Gametime will become a year-round destination spanning “all professional sports.” However, that full expansion isn’t yet live as of today. Instead, X announced its second league partnership with Major League Baseball (MLB).
