India rejects Elon Musk's claim of discrimination over Starlink launch | TechCrunch

India has rejected Elon Musk’s claim that Starlink, SpaceX’s satellite internet service, is being unfairly blocked from launching in the country. SpaceX first attempted to launch Starlink in India in 2021.
