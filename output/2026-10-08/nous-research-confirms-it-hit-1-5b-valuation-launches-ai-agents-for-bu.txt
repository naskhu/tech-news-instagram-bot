Nous Research confirms it hit $1.5B valuation, launches AI agents for business users | TechCrunch

Nous Research , the startup developing the open source Hermes Agent, has raised a $90 million Series B at a $1.5 billion valuation, confirming TechCrunch’s earlier reporting . The new financing brings the three-year-old startup’s total funding to $158 million.
