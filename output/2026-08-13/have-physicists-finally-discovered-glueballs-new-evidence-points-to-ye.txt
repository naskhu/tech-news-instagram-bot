Have physicists finally discovered glueballs? New evidence points to yes.

Physicists with the Beijing Spectrometer III (BES III) experiment have uncovered convincing new evidence of the existence of so-called glueballs , an elusive composite particle made entirely of gluons predicted by quantum theory.
