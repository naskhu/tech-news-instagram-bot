Amazon will train on Twitch streamers’ content by default, unless they opt out

The streaming platform Twitch will now use creators’ content to help train generative AI models for its parent company, Amazon. For Amazon, these stream recordings are incredibly valuable, offering thousands of hours of audio and video content to help train AI models.
