An AI-supervised remote exam went so badly that 58,000 students must retake it

Earlier this summer, nearly 160,000 applicants took the entrance exam for UNAM, Mexico’s largest university. Between 2021 and 2025, 3.5 percent of test takers scored 100 or more on the 120-question UNAM test.
