AWS is helping vibe-coding startup Superblocks, and the implications are big | TechCrunch

Vibe coding startup Superblocks announced a multi-year joint marketing agreement with Amazon Web Services (AWS) that enables its tool to be embedded within the private clouds of AWS customers. Amazon also has an AI assistant, Quick, for business users.
