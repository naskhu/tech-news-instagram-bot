Is the future of data centers portable? Runware builds a pod to find out | TechCrunch

On Tuesday, AI infrastructure company Runware announced the launch of its own modular data center called Sonic Inference Pod. The modular design means it’s easy add capacity quickly by creating new pods rather than having to expand a fixed data center.
