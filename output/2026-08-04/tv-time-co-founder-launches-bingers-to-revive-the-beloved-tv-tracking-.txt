TV Time co-founder launches Bingers to revive the beloved TV-tracking app

Bingers, a new TV show and movie-tracking app, has arrived to fill the hole that TV Time’s closure left. Like TV Time, fans can create profiles and comment on shows and movies, including by adding media like photos and GIFs.
