Snap reports 971 million monthly active users ahead of its Specs launch event on September 16th.

That’s up from 932 million a year ago, according to Snap’s latest earnings report . The company also says its revenue is up 19 percent year-over-year, as it prepares to launch its $2,195 augmented-reality glasses next month.
