Crusoe abandons $1.25B plan to use Boom turbines at AI data centers | TechCrunch

Crusoe, a Denver-based AI data center startup that recently raised $3.9 billion , has ended plans to use a new line of stationary power plants developed by fellow Denver company Boom Supersonic.
