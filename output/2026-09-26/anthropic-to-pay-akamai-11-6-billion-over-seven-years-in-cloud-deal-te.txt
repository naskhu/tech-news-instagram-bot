Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years in cloud deal | TechCrunch

Anthropic will spend $11.6 billion over seven years on Akamai’s cloud infrastructure, Akamai said Thursday . The deal is the largest in Akamai’s history and continues Anthropic’s compute gobbling-streak .
