Google's AI search is rapidly becoming the default, new data shows | TechCrunch

In just a year’s time, Google’s AI-generated answers in search, known as AI Overviews , have gone from appearing in 15% of searches to 43%, according to a new report, driving a shift in how web users consume information online.
