iOS and macOS 26.6 arrive today, paving the way for iOS and macOS 27

Apple has released iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and tvOS 26.6. Apple also rolled out macOS 14.8.8 and macOS 15.7.8 for older devices, also focused on security fixes.
