It’s Becoming Clear Why Black Holes Never Run Out of Fuel

As a black hole emits jets and heats the surrounding gas, the gas that would otherwise be consumed by the black hole—that is, its energy source—is dispersed far away. These filaments then fall back toward the black hole.
