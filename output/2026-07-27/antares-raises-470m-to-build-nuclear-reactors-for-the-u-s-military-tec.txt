Antares raises $470M to build nuclear reactors for the U.S. military | TechCrunch

Nuclear power startup Antares said Monday that it has raised $470 million to build small reactors for U.S. military bases. Like many other advanced nuclear startups, Antares’s reactor uses TRISO fuel, which encapsulates uranium in carbon and ceramic shells.
