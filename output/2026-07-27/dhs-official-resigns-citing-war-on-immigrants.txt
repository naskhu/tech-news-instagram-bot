DHS Official Resigns, Citing ‘War on Immigrants’

The Department of Homeland Security’s top numbers-cruncher has resigned, citing the Trump administration’s “war on immigrants” on his way out. Same goes for Kristi Noem—Trump’s first DHS chief, who left under a cloud of scandal—and her controversial top aide, Corey Lewandowski.
