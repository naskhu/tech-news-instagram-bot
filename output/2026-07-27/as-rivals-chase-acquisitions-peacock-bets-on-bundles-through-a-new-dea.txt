As rivals chase acquisitions, Peacock bets on bundles through a new deal with YouTube

On Monday, NBCUniversal and YouTube announced a multi-year global strategic partnership, bringing Peacock’s Premium plan to YouTube Premium subscribers in the U.S. starting in early 2027. For YouTube, the partnership makes YouTube Premium a stronger offering by adding another major entertainment service.
