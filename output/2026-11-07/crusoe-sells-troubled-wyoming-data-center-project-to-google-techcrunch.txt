Crusoe sells troubled Wyoming data center project to Google | TechCrunch

Crusoe, an eight-year-old, Denver-based data center developer recently valued at $30.9 billion , confirmed Friday it has sold an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google. Crusoe is developing another data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas.
