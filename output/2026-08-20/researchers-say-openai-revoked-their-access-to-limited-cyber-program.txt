Researchers say OpenAI revoked their access to limited cyber program

On Wednesday, multiple researchers on OpenAI’s official support forums and on X reported having their access to the Trusted Access for Cyber (TAC) program revoked. To get access to TAC, cybersecurity researchers have to submit an ID and be vetted by OpenAI.
