Coders Say They Already Found Workarounds to Claude’s Invisible Watermarks

Within four hours of Anthropic confirming that Claude models would globally embed invisible, machine-readable watermarks into any AI-generated content, developer Guillaume Meyer had published his override. Anthropic itself acknowledged that heavily edited, paraphrased, or translated content might not carry a watermark.
