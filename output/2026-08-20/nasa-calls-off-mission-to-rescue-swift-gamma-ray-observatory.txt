NASA calls off mission to rescue Swift gamma-ray observatory

Katalyst, the startup leading the Swift rescue effort, said Wednesday that “ongoing attitude control issues” will prevent the Link satellite from completing its rescue mission. Swift is falling out of orbit and is expected to reenter the atmosphere later this year.
