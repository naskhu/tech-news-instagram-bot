Rillet raises $100M Series C at $1B valuation — 2 years after emerging from stealth

AI accounting startup Rillet announced a $100 million Series C at a $1 billion valuation on Tuesday, led by Iconiq . Shortly before that round, it announced a $25 million Series A led by Sequoia.
