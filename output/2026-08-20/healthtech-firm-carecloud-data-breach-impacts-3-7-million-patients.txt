Healthtech firm CareCloud data breach impacts 3.7 million patients

U.S. healthcare IT company CareCloud disclosed that the data breach incident it suffered earlier this year has impacted more than 3.7 million individuals. Following the incident, CareCloud launched an investigation to determine its scope and how many people were potentially impacted.
