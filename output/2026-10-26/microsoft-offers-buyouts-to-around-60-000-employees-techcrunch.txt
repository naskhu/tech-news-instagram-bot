Microsoft offers buyouts to around 60,000 employees | TechCrunch

Microsoft is offering buyouts to around 60,000 employees, the company confirmed to TechCrunch on Monday. Microsoft’s Chief People Officer, Amy Coleman, sent employees a note about the buyouts, which was also published on the company’s website.
