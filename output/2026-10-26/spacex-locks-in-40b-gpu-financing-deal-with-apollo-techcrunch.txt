SpaceX locks in $40B GPU financing deal with Apollo | TechCrunch

Apollo said the financing will help deliver Nvidia’s next-generation Vera Rubin chips for SpaceX’s “terrestrial compute buildout.” The financing comes as SpaceX ramps spending to expand its AI infrastructure, alongside its Starlink and launch businesses.
