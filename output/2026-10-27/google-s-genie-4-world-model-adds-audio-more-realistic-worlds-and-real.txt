Google’s Genie 4 world model adds audio, more realistic worlds, and real-time steering | TechCrunch

Google DeepMind released its newest world model, Genie 4, in research preview on Tuesday. In a research experiment tied to Gemini Robotics, a Gemini 4 Argon model broke down tasks into steps that Genie 4 simulated in real time.
