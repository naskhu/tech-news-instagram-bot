Tesla turns to Turo to boost adoption of Full Self-Driving (Supervised) | TechCrunch

Eligible Turo “hosts” who rent their cars on the platform will also receive a free one-month trial of FSD. Turo said it is working with Tesla to directly access the automaker’s API to verify which cars have FSD enabled.
