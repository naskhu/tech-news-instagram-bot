Apple unveils new iPad mini with OLED display and faster A20 Pro chip | TechCrunch

Apple on Monday announced a new iPad mini with a larger 8.5-inch OLED display. The iPad mini also comes with a new nano-texture display option that reduces glare in bright environments, a feature already available on the iPad Pro.
