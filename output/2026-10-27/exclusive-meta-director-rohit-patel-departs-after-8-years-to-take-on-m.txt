Exclusive: Meta director Rohit Patel departs after 8 years to take on Microsoft Office Suite

The former director at Meta Superintelligence Labs has launched Ndoc , a startup he’s pitching as an AI-native replacement for Microsoft Office suite or Google Workspace. Patel also says Ndoc is built and fine-tuned using a mixture of open source models.
