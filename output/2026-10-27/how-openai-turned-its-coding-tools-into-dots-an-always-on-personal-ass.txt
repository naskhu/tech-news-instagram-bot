How OpenAI turned its coding tools into dots, an always-on personal assistant | TechCrunch

With dots, OpenAI says that the agent has the same privacy controls as ChatGPT itself: Users can turn off model training (stopping OpenAI from using their conversations to improve its AI), and control what resources it accesses within your computer and how.
