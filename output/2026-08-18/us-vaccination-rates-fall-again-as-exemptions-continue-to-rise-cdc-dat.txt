US vaccination rates fall again as exemptions continue to rise, CDC data shows

Nationally, only 92.4 percent of kindergartners were vaccinated with the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine in the 2025–2026 school year, down from 92.5 percent last year. Currently, 24 states have exemption rates at or above 5 percent.
