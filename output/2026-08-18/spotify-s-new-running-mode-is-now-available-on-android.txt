Spotify’s new Running Mode is now available on Android.

The feature, which initially launched on iOS , offers preset playlists for running. It’s available to Premium subscribers in the US, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and Sweden on both mobile platforms, Spotify says.
