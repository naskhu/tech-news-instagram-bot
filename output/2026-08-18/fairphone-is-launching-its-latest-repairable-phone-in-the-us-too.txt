Fairphone is launching its latest repairable phone in the US too

Fairphone, the Dutch company known for its sustainable and repair-friendly phones, on Tuesday said its latest smartphone, Fairphone (Gen 6+), is now available in the U.S, starting at $649. The company said the Fairphone 6+ will support T-Mobile and AT&T at launch.
