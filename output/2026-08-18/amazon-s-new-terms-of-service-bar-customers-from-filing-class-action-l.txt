Amazon’s New Terms of Service Bar Customers From Filing Class-Action Lawsuits - CNET

Amazon’s updated terms of service now contain a clause that may take many subscribers by surprise. Before any arbitration proceeding can be filed, customers must submit a dispute claim to Amazon itself, giving the company 60 days to resolve it.
