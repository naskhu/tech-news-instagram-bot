Spotify’s new Playlist Notes let users and editors explain their song picks | TechCrunch

Spotify announced Monday the launch of “Playlist Notes,” a new feature that lets listeners add their thoughts to the playlists they make. With Playlist Notes, users can add context to songs, podcast episodes, and audiobooks in their playlists.
