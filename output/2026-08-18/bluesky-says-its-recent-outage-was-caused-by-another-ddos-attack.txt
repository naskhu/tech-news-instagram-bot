Bluesky says its recent outage was caused by another DDoS attack

Social networking site Bluesky says its recent day-long disruption was caused by a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack that flooded the site with junk traffic. This is the latest large-scale DDoS attack to affect the social networking site in recent months.
