As temperatures get hotter, pesticides are more dangerous to farmworkers

Excess heat affects the body’s ability to regulate its internal temperature, which can lead to heat cramps, heat exhaustion, and heatstroke. There are few federal or state regulatory protections for farmworkers from heat.
