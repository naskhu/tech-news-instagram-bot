Snapchat surpasses 30M paid subscribers | TechCrunch

Snap has surpassed 30 million paid Snapchat+ subscribers, the company announced Wednesday in its third-quarter 2026 earnings release . Snap reported 495 million daily active users for the quarter, up from 493 million in the previous quarter.
