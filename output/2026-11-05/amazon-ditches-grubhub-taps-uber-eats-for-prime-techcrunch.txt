Amazon ditches GrubHub, taps Uber Eats for Prime | TechCrunch

Under the new partnership announced Thursday, Amazon Prime members will be able to order food from Uber Eats directly through Amazon.com and the Amazon Shopping app. Amazon’s ouster of Grubhub ends a once-close relationship.
