Google Search’s AI Mode can now tap into Drive and Wallet for more personalized responses

Google announced on Monday that it’s expanding Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode with integrations for Google Drive, Google Wallet, and the Gemini app. The feature can already connect to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Photos.
