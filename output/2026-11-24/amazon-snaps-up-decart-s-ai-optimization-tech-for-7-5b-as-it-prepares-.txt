Amazon snaps up Decart's AI optimization tech for $7.5B as it prepares to sell Trainium chips | TechCrunch

Amazon has agreed to acquire a good chunk of Israeli startup Decart. Decart’s technology could make it easier for customers to run their workloads on Amazon chips, rather than Nvidia’s, which has been the gold standard so far.
