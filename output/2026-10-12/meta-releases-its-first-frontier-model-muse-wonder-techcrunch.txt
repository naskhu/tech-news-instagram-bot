Meta releases its first frontier model, Muse Wonder | TechCrunch

Meta’s Superintelligence Lab released a pair of new state-of-the-art models on Monday, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, representing the company’s first attempt at a frontier model. Both models will also be used to power the Muse agent.
