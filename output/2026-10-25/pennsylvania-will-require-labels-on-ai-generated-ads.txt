Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads

Pennsylvania’s AI Deceptive Advertising Act leaves a lot of loopholes. On Thursday, Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act into law, requiring disclosures on AI-generated ads.
