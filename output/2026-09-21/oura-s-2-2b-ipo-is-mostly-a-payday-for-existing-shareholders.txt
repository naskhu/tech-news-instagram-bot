Oura’s $2.2B IPO is mostly a payday for existing shareholders

According to the company’s updated IPO filing, Oura and its shareholders are together offering 50 million shares at a price range of $40 to $44 each. It had raised $200 million at a $5.2 billion valuation less than a year earlier.
