Reliance's JioHotstar takes its streaming empire global — without sports | TechCrunch

The streaming service will launch with more than 160,000 hours of entertainment content but without the live sports that have helped drive its massive audience in India, including the Indian Premier League (IPL), Women’s Premier League (WPL), and Indian national cricket matches.
