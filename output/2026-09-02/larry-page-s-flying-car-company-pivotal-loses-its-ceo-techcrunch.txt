Larry Page's flying car company Pivotal loses its CEO | TechCrunch

Ken Karklin, who was Pivotal’s CEO for more than four years, left the role this week, according to a press release . Karklin’s departure comes two years after Pivotal started sales of Helix , its first lightweight electric personal aircraft.
