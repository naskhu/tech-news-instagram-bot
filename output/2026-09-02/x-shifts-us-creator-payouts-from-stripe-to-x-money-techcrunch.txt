X shifts US creator payouts from Stripe to X Money | TechCrunch

Elon Musk’s social network X on Wednesday said that starting today, all of its U.S. creator payouts will be handled through X Money, the app’s payments service. The company’s new payments service, X Money , began supporting creator payouts earlier this month.
