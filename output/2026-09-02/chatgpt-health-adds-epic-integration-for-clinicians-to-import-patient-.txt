ChatGPT Health adds Epic integration for clinicians to import patient data | TechCrunch

OpenAI said today that it is integrating ChatGPT Health with Epic’s electronic health record (EHR) system, which holds data for over 325 million patients , to let clinicians import patient data and use AI to ask questions.
