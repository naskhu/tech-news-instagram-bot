Adobe is making its tools available in Slack | TechCrunch

Customers can now use Adobe’s apps like Firefly, Adobe Express, Photoshop, Premiere, Acrobat, InDesign, Illustrator, Stock, Lightroom, and others directly with Slack’s AI chatbot, Slackbot, Adobe announced on Monday. At launch, this integration will be available to Slack Business+ and Enterprise+ teams.
