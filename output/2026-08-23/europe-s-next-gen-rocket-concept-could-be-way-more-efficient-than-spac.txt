Europe's Next-Gen Rocket Concept Could Be Way More Efficient Than SpaceX's Starship - BGR

Germany's Aerospace Center (DLR) published a paper in May 2025 proposing a more efficient European version of SpaceX's Starship rocket, with the concept called the RLV C5. This SpaceLiner booster concept has been in development since 2005.
