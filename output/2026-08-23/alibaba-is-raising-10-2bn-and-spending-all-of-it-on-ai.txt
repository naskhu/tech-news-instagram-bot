Alibaba is raising $10.2bn and spending all of it on AI

Alibaba is placing HK$80bn, about $10.2bn, of new shares in Hong Kong and says all net proceeds will go to AI infrastructure and capabilities. Only Alphabet, which raised $85bn in equity , and Intel have run larger primary follow-ons in 2026.
