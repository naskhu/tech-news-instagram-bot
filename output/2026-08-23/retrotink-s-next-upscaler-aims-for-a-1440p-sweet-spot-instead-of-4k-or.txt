Retrotink’s next upscaler aims for a 1440p sweet spot, instead of 4K or 1080p.

Rising component prices and wild tariff swings are still an issue, so Mike Chi of Retrotink has designed a new hardware upscaler for retro gaming . The new Retrotink 6X CE starts at $230 and starts shipping next week.
