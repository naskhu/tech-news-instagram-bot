Is a liquid-cooled PC worth it? - Engadget

Air coolers use a heatsink and pipes to regulate a processor's temperature, whereas liquid systems use coolant. A liquid cooler regulates the heat of a CPU by directing heat away from the processor via a water block, cooling pump and a radiator.
