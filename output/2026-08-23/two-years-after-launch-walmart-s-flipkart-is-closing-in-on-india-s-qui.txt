Two years after launch, Walmart's Flipkart is closing in on India's quick-commerce leaders | TechCrunch

Flipkart Minutes, which debuted in August 2024 as the e-commerce giant’s foray into quick commerce, is now delivering 1.1 million to 1.2 million orders a day, up from about 390,000 to 400,000 in November, people familiar with the matter told TechCrunch.
