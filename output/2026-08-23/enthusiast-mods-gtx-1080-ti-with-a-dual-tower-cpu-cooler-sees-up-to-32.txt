Enthusiast mods GTX 1080 Ti with a dual-tower CPU cooler, sees up to 32°C reduction in temps — Mod yields 30W power savings and 9% higher FPS

The result was a graphics card 9°C cooler than before and with slightly better clocks. The first run saw the stock 1060 cooler peg the card's hotspot at 72°C while the dual-tower cooler sat at just 42°C — that's 30°C cooler!
