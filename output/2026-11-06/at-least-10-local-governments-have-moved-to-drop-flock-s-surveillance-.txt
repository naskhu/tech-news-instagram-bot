At least 10 local governments have moved to drop Flock’s surveillance cameras since summer | TechCrunch

In Asheville, North Carolina, police lost access to Flock after the city voted to terminate its contract in August, and the El Paso County Sheriff’s Office in Colorado announced in September that it would stop using Flock cameras.
