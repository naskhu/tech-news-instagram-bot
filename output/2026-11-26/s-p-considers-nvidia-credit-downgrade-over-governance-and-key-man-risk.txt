S&P considers Nvidia credit downgrade over governance and 'key man' risks | TechCrunch

S&P Global Ratings, one of the three major credit rating agencies, is considering downgrading Nvidia’s rating, citing concerns about the company’s management and governance practices. Although Nvidia is certain to keep its investment-grade rating, a downgrade could raise its borrowing costs.
