Anthropic’s Dario Amodei calls accusations of surrendering human control to AI ‘absurd’ | TechCrunch

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is pushing back against claims that Anthropic believes we should “surrender human free will” to AI systems. Amodei suggested drawing a line at recursive self-improvement (RSI), or AI that can improve itself without human assistance.
