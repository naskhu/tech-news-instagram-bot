Spotify users on iOS 27 can now ask Siri to play music without having to say 'on Spotify' | TechCrunch

Spotify says removing the extra voice command will make hands-free listening feel more natural, as users can just focus on what they want to hear, not how to phrase the request properly to get their preferred music app to respond.
