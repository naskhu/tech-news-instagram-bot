Nvidia partners with data center developer Cloverleaf

On Friday, it announced a partnership with Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a company that lays the groundwork for data centers. Nvidia is increasingly playing a more direct role in financing and developing the AI data centers that turn around and buy its AI systems.
