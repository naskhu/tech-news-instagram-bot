Nokia's Last Two Smartphones Are Set To Lose Software Support Soon - BGR

The Nokia G310 5G is losing software support on August 24, 2026, and the Nokia XR21 is losing software support on May 24, 2027. These phones were designed and manufactured by HMD, but bore the familiar Nokia logo.
