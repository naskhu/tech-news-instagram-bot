Motorola's GrapheneOS phones will launch in 2027 priced higher than Pixels

Several months ago, Motorola and the makers of GrapheneOS announced a team-up that would expand support for the privacy-centric OS to beyond Google’s Pixel phones. Moto’s GrapheneOS phones are on track to launch in 2027, but they’ll cost a pretty penny.
