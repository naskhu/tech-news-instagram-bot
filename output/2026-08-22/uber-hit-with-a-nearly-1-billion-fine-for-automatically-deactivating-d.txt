Uber hit with a nearly $1 billion fine for automatically deactivating drivers in Europe - Engadget

A Dutch data regulatory authority said that Uber has to pay 824.9 million euros for violating the GDPR. According to the Dutch regulatory agency, Uber has deprived its drivers of income through fully automated decisions, violating the GDPR in the process.
