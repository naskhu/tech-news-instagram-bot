An okay laptop with 16GB of RAM is better than a nice laptop with 8GB, and this $520 HP OmniBook proves it

The OmniBook’s 8-core Snapdragon X processor is a little slower than the one in the $950 Surface Laptop I tested, but the OmniBook’s day-to-day performance is better because it’s not starved for RAM and its larger SSD is a little faster.
