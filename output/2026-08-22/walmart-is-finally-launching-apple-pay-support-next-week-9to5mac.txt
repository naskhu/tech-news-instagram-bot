Walmart is finally launching Apple Pay support next week - 9to5Mac

Walmart is adding broad contactless payment support, so that includes Apple Pay on iPhone and Apple Watch, Google Pay, contactless cards, and other payment platforms. Contactless support will exist alongside existing payment methods, including Walmart Pay.
