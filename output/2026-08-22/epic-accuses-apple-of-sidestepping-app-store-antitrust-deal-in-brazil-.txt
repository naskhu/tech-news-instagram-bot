Epic accuses Apple of sidestepping App Store antitrust deal in Brazil - 9to5Mac

Apple has until Monday to respond to a complaint Epic Games filed with Brazil’s antitrust watchdog, where it accuses the company of adopting restrictive interpretations of its obligations under a recent App Store antitrust deal in the country.
