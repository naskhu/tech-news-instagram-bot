US battery startups have found a lifeline in defense

Defense applications of lithium-ion batteries are “absolutely playing out in discussions,” a spokesperson for Coreshell , a battery materials startup, told TechCrunch. Defense Logistics Agency was buying $200 million worth of batteries annually.
