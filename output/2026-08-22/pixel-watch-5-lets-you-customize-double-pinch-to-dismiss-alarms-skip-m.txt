Pixel Watch 5 lets you customize double pinch to dismiss alarms, skip music, & more

Besides expanded availability across apps, the double pinch gesture on the Pixel Watch 5 benefits from being able to “Customize actions” across music and alarms. On the Pixel Watch 4, you can only snooze an alarm with the double pinch gesture.
