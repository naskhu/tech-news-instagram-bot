TikTok's US arm joins Lantern, a cross-platform child safety initiative | TechCrunch

Eight months after it was spun out of TikTok , the social media platform’s U.S. unit, known as TikTok USDS Joint Venture, is joining Lantern, a cross-platform child safety program by a group of tech companies.
