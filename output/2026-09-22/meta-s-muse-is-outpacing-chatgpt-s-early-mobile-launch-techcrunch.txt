Meta's Muse is outpacing ChatGPT’s early mobile launch | TechCrunch

Meta’s latest AI app, Muse, could be the social giant’s next major hit, new data suggests. When comparing just the U.S. mobile app daily active users, Muse comes in higher with 642,000 daily users compared with 231,000 for ChatGPT at the time.
