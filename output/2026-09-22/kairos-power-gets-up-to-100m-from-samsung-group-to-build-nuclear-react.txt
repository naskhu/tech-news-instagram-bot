Kairos Power gets up to $100M from Samsung group to build nuclear reactor for Google | TechCrunch

Kairos Power has picked engineering firm Samsung C&T to help build its 50-megawatt demonstration reactor for Google , which the nuclear startup is aiming to have completed by 2030. The second, Hermes 2, is Kairos’ first commercial-scale reactor.
