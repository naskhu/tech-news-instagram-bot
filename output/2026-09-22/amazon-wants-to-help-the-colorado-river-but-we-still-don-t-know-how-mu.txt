Amazon wants to help the Colorado River, but we still don’t know how much water the company uses

The Colorado River faces an ‘unprecedented’ drought as data centers demand more water. Amazon’s annual sustainability report shares water use per unit of power (l/kwh) for its data centers since 2021, but not the company’s total water use over that time period.
