Apple launches new app to study how people use AI for health | TechCrunch

The new app will allow users to ask health-related questions, as they do with ChatGPT or Claude, with answers grounded in clinical data, from sources including The New England Journal of Medicine, Journal of the American Medical Association, and Nature.
