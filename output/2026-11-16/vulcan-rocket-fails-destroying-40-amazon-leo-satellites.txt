Vulcan rocket fails, destroying 40 Amazon Leo satellites

ULA said an ‘anomaly’ led to its Vulcan vehicle failing to deliver its payload to orbit. United Launch Alliance (ULA) suffered what is reportedly its first mission failure in its 20-year history, failing to deliver 40 Amazon Leo satellites to orbit.
