YouTube Music launches new labels as AI-generated music floods streaming platforms | TechCrunch

YouTube said it collaborated with various music partners and deployed its own detection tools to identify AI-generated music. The platform labels detected AI-generated music and excludes it from algorithmic recommendations and editorial playlists, while also withholding royalties from fraudulent streams.
