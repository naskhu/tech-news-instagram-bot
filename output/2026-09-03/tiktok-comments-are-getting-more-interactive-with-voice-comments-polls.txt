TikTok comments are getting more interactive with voice comments, polls, and more | TechCrunch

TikTok’s comments section is getting an upgrade. The social network announced on Thursday that it’s rolling out new features for its comment sections, including voice comments, comment polls, photo carousel comments, and Live Photo comments.
