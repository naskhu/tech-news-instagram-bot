Circular unveils Ring 3 series with contactless payments and on-finger vibration alerts | TechCrunch

Smart ring maker Circular on Thursday unveiled its upcoming Ring 3 series, which consists of a Pro model and a Slim option. As with the Ring 2, the new smart rings will be available in gold, rose gold, silver, and black.
