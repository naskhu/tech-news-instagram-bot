Amazon's Zoox expands its robotaxi service to Las Vegas airport | TechCrunch

The company has extended its commercial robotaxi service in Las Vegas to include rides to and from Harry Reid International Airport. Zoox is the only robotaxi company operating a service to the Las Vegas airport, but more competition is coming.
