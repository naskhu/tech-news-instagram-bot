Uber beats Waymo as first to launch robotaxis in London

The vehicles use autonomous driving tech developed by Wayve , a UK-based startup, and will initially feature safety drivers behind the wheel. In addition to Uber, Wayve is also working with Stellantis on hands-free driving tech.
