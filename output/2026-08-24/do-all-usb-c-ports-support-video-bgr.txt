Do All USB-C Ports Support Video? - BGR

Not every USB-C port or cable supports video output. USB-C docks can take an incoming USB-C video signal and route it to the devices plugged into its dedicated video ports, such as DisplayPort, VGA, HDMI, and also USB-C.
