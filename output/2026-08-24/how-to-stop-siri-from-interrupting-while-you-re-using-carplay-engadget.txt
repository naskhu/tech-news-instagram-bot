How to stop Siri from interrupting while you're using CarPlay - Engadget

Another way to prevent stoppages with Siri is hidden under Settings > Accessibility > Siri > Require "Siri" for Interruptions on your phone. Then, Siri won't stop what it's saying unless you say "Siri" to prompt it again.
