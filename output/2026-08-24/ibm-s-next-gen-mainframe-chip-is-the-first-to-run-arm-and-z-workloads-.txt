IBM’s next-gen mainframe chip is the first to run Arm and Z workloads on the same cores

A computer-aided design layout of IBM's new processor, the first mainframe chip capable of natively running both IBM's instruction set and Arm's. For IBM's installed base, the reflexive question is whether embracing Arm signals a slow sunset for the traditional architecture.
