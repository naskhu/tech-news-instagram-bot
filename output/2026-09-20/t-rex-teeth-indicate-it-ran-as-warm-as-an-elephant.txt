T. rex teeth indicate it ran as warm as an elephant

Since tooth enamel forms inside a living body, the number of those clumped bonds records the animal’s body temperature, regardless of the water content in its body. Averaged together, T. rex had a body temperature of 36.3°, give or take 2.5°.
