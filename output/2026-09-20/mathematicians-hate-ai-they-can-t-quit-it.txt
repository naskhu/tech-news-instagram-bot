Mathematicians Hate AI. They Can’t Quit It

Mathematician Tristan Buckmaster believes OpenAI used his work to rush ahead and beat him to solving a legendary math problem with a $1 million bounty. Showing that AI was pushing the boundaries of mathematics was “more important than the result,” Buckmaster says.
