Google froze its open source bug bounty program due to a 'significant rise' in AI submissions | TechCrunch

Blaming a “significant rise” in AI submissions, Google has paused its open source bug bounty program until next year. In the meantime, participants are encouraged to consider Google’s other bug bounty programs.
