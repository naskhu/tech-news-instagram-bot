Volvo’s plug-in hybrid XC60 and XC90 can really go the distance

The automaker’s updated PHEVs have bigger batteries, giving them the longest electric range of any plug-in hybrid on the market, Volvo says. The XC60 is getting new styling to bring it inline with Volvo’s new design language.
