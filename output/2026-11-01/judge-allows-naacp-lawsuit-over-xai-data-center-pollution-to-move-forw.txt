Judge allows NAACP lawsuit over xAI data center pollution to move forward | TechCrunch

A federal judge denied a motion by xAI seeking to dismiss an NAACP lawsuit accusing the SpaceX-owned company of failing to obtain permits needed to operate the gas turbines powering its Colossus data center outside Memphis.
