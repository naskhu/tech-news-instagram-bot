Binance CEO says 90 percent of stolen assets have been ‘replenished’

After hackers stole a shocking $6.3 billion from Binance customers in a record-breaking crypto heist, the company’s CEO says that 90 percent of the funds have been replaced , and the company plans to cover the remaining 10 percent as well.
