FBI, Coast Guard boarded hacked oil tankers heading toward US coast | TechCrunch

Coast Guard and the FBI boarded two U.S.-bound oil tankers last month after hackers reportedly compromised at least one of the ship’s networks and took control of its navigation, propulsion, and cargo systems.
