Apple Wallet driver's license: which states are next? - 9to5Mac

At WWDC 2021, Apple announced support for storing your driver’s license in Apple Wallet on iPhone. Right now, iPhone and Apple Watch users in 14 states and Puerto Rico can add their driver’s license or state ID to Apple Wallet.
