Against all odds, SpaceX finally tugs Starship into port after 24 days at sea

SpaceX’s most recent Starship test flight may have ended July 24, but its mission isn’t over yet. Starship’s heat shield, made of approximately 18,000 ceramic tiles, is key to SpaceX’s goal of making the vehicle fully and rapidly reusable.
