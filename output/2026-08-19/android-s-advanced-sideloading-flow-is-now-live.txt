Android’s “advanced” sideloading flow is now live.

Google’s Mishaal Rahman took to r/Android to announce the process, which will eventually be the only way to install apps from unverified Android developers. Enabling the one-time advanced flow now gets the mandatory 24-hour cooldown out of the way.
