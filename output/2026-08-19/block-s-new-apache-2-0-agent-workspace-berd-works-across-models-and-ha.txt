Block’s new Apache 2.0 agent workspace Berd works across models and harnesses, stores conversation history locally

Architecturally, Berd is neither a new foundation model nor a new agent runtime. Goose runs quietly alongside Berd as a bundled “sidecar” process and handles the underlying agent loop; Berd handles projects, sessions, context, agents and configuration on top.
