Amazon’s Prime Air is taking off in nearly 500 U.S. cities | TechCrunch

Amazon announced Wednesday a major expansion of Amazon Prime Air, its drone delivery service, with plans to reach nearly 500 U.S. cities by the end of 2026. Amazon’s push comes as other major companies continue building their own drone delivery networks.
