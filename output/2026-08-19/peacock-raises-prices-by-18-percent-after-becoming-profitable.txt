Peacock raises prices by 18 percent after becoming profitable

The monthly fee for the standard ad plan (Premium) went from $11/month to $13/month, and the ad-free plan (Premium Plus) went from $17/month to $20/month. Peacock also raised prices in July 2024 and August 2023.
