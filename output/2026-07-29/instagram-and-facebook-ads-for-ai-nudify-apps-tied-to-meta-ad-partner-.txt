Instagram and Facebook Ads for AI 'Nudify' Apps Tied to Meta Ad Partner in China - CNET

Thousands of ads for “nudify” apps on Meta-owned Facebook and Instagram have been traced back to GatherOne, one of Meta’s 11 Chinese advertising partners , according to a report published on Sunday by the Tech Transparency Project.
