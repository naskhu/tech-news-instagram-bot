DoorDash is building its own drone delivery business | TechCrunch

DoorDash Air was developed within DoorDash Labs, the R&D team behind Dot, the autonomous sidewalk delivery bot the company introduced in September 2025. In the company’s view, drones and sidewalk bots are part of the broader delivery network it is building.
