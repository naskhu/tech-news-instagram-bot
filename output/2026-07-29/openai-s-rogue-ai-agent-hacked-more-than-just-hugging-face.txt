OpenAI’s Rogue AI Agent Hacked More Than Just Hugging Face

OpenAI said Tuesday that the rogue AI agent that breached Hugging Face’s platform also hacked multiple third-party accounts and services as part of the attack. Hugging Face’s forensic team concluded that OpenAI’s agent was essentially trying to cheat on ExploitGym’s test.
