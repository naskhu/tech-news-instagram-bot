Amazon is trying to crush class-action suits before they get started

An update to its terms and conditions requires all disputes be resolved through arbitration. Rather than let those sorts of cases go to court, Amazon’s new terms would seek to resolve larger disputes through mass arbitration rather than mandatory 1-on-1 arbitration hearings.
