You might want to skip Newegg's GPU trade-in program - Engadget

Newegg's GPU Trade-In program makes upgrading surprisingly painless. Newegg handles the shipping with a prepaid UPS label, gives customers 14 days to send their old component and returns rejected hardware without charging for return shipping.
