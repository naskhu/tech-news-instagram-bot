Large-scale DDoS attacks disrupted Threema secure messaging service

Multiple distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks targeted the Threema secure messaging service earlier this week, causing severe disruptions to communications. ​Organizations using Threema On-Prem did not experience any issues because they rely on their own infrastructure.
