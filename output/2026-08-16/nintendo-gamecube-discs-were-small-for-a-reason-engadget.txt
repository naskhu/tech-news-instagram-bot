Nintendo GameCube discs were small for a reason - Engadget

Sometimes known as "GameCube Optical Discs", they were significantly smaller than standard DVDs. Though modders would eventually crack GameCube emulation with the Dolphin emulator, it was much harder to pirate GameCube titles than PS2 or Xbox games in the 2000s.
