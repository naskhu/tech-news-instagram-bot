Google Will Allow Users to Remove Watermarks From Gemini's Output

Users will now have the option to remove this visible watermark from images, video, and music generated by Google Gemini's Nano Banana, Omni, and Lyria tools. We've already seen the introduction of watermarks stir up controversy among users.
