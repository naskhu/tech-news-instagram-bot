How To Enable Right Click On Your Mac Trackpad Or Magic Mouse - BGR

When it comes to the Mac trackpad or the Magic Mouse, there's one function new users often miss. If you're using a Magic Mouse, visit the same System Settings app on your Mac, select Mouse , and choose Secondary click .
