Nvidia in Talks to Invest $3 Billion in SB Energy as Part of OpenAI Data Center Deal

Nvidia is in talks to invest as much as $3 billion in SB Energy, the Softbank Group-backed developer of a massive planned Ohio data center project for OpenAI, according to people familiar with the discussions.
