The US Navy finally has a carrier with a built-in center for drones and other unmanned aircraft

According to a Pentagon acquisition document dated April 21, 2026, the Theodore Roosevelt “became the first fully operational/deployable MQ-25 capable aircraft carrier.” The development gives the carrier force a dedicated infrastructure for unmanned aviation rather than treating drones as temporary additions.
