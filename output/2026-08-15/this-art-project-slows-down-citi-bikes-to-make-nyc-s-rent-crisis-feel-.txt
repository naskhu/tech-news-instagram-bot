This Art Project Slows Down Citi Bikes to Make NYC’s Rent Crisis Feel Real

When the bike passes a block the dataset says has a higher percentage of rent insecurity, the microcontroller triggers a small motor that applies the brake to add resistance, and the bike gets physically harder to pedal.
