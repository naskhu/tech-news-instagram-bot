Android 17 QPR2 greatly expands Dynamic Color theming on Pixel

With Android 17 QPR2 Beta 3’s surprise Friday drop, Google is introducing expanded Dynamic Color theming options for Pixel devices. The paint palette icon above the carousel lets you access a “Select a color” slider to choose the main accent.
