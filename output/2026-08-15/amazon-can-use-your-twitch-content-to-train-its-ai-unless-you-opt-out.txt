Amazon Can Use Your Twitch Content to Train Its AI—Unless You Opt Out

Twitch has updated its account settings to let streamers opt out of having their content used to help train the artificial intelligence models of Twitch’s parent company, Amazon. This story originally appeared in WIRED en Español and has been translated from Spanish.
