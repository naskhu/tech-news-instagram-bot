Google’s Pixel Phones Are Getting a Tap-to-Share Feature for Contact Info and More

Google’s Quick Share now has a tap-to-share feature that lets you do the same motion to swap files between phones. You’ll need to open your phone’s Share Sheet before tapping the devices together if you want to share photos and videos.
