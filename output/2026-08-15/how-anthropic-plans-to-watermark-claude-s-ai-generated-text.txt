How Anthropic plans to watermark Claude's AI-generated text

Anthropic says future Claude models will generate watermarked text. Such modifications introduce a statistical signature into the generated text; during the watermark detection phase, the signature can be measured to determine whether the text was indeed generated by the watermarked LLM.
