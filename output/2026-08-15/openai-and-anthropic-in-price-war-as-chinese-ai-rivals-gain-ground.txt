OpenAI and Anthropic in price war as Chinese AI rivals gain ground

Tokens are the units of data processed by language models and are used to calculate many customers’ bills. Anthropic launched Opus 5 at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens—half the price of its Fable 5 model.
