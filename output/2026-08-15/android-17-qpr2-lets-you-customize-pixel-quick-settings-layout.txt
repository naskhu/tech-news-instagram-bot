Android 17 QPR2 lets you customize Pixel Quick Settings layout

The surprise Friday drop of Android 17 QPR2 lets you customize the layout of the Quick Settings shade on Pixel. Open Quick Settings and tap the pencil icon in the corner for a new “Layout” tab at the bottom of your screen.
