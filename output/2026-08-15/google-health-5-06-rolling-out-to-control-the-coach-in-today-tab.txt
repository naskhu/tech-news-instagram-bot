Google Health 5.06 rolling out to control the Coach in Today tab

Google Health 5.06 is rolling out on Android and iOS to let you control what Health Coach guidance appears in the Today tab. Since launch, the Google Health Coach has shown “proactive, conversational insights and daily summaries” in the Today tab.
