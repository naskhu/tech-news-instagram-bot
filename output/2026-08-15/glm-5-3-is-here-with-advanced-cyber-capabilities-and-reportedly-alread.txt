GLM-5.3 is here with advanced cyber capabilities — and reportedly already found a 'serious vulnerability' in Cursor

Z.ai says GLM-5.3 uses the same base model as GLM-5.2, with the improvements coming entirely from scaling post-training across more environments, more diverse tasks and additional reinforcement-learning compute. GLM-5.3 builds on the 743-billion-parameter-scale base model behind GLM-5.2 rather than replacing it.
