YouTube’s new ‘Shorts series’ feature brings episodic viewing to Shorts | TechCrunch

At its Made On YouTube event on Wednesday, the company announced “Shorts series,” a new tool that lets creators organize their Shorts into TV-style seasons and episodes. The series will live on a creator’s YouTube channel.
