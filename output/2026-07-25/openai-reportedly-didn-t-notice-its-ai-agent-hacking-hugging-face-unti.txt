OpenAI reportedly didn’t notice its AI agent hacking Hugging Face until a week later.

According to Reuters , the AI agent that went looking for ExploitGym hacking benchmark shortcuts on Hugging Face’s systems started trying to escape its not-sandboxed-well-enough test environment around July 9th, and the actual intrusion lasted from the 11th until the 13th.
