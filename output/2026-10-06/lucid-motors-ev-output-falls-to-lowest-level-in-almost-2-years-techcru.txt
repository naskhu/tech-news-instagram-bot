Lucid Motors' EV output falls to lowest level in almost 2 years | TechCrunch

This was the third straight quarter in which the number of EVs Lucid built has declined. Lucid delivered 3,806 EVs in the third quarter, roughly flat with the second quarter and down about 200 vehicles from the third quarter of 2025.
