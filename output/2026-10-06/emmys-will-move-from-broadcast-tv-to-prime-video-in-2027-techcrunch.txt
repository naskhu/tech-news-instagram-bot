Emmys will move from broadcast TV to Prime Video in 2027 | TechCrunch

Amazon announced on Tuesday that Prime Video will be the new home for the Emmy Awards, marking another major Hollywood awards show’s move from broadcast television to streaming. The Emmys aren’t the only major awards show making the shift to streaming.
