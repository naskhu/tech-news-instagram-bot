OpenAI launches visual ads that appear alongside image generation results | TechCrunch

Specifically, OpenAI is adding visual display ads to its ad product line-up so that ads will appear alongside images that users ask ChatGPT to generate. OpenAI has also partnered with Haus, Measured, and WorkMagic to help advertisers explore “geo-based” experiments around ads.
