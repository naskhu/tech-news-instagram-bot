Etched fields funding offers at $40B+ valuation, sources say | TechCrunch

Etched is reviewing incoming bids that range from $40 billion from top-tier investors to $50 billion from lesser-known backers, one person said. The startup announced a $300 million round at a $10.3 billion valuation led by Sequoia in July.
