Discord is making its public communities more searchable, and people have concerns | TechCrunch

The new update targets conversations in “Discoverable Servers,” public Discord servers that administrators have made eligible to appear in the platform’s Server Discovery feature. Private servers, direct messages, and group direct messages are excluded.
