Free Fire returns to India as Garena launches localized version four years after ban | TechCrunch

Vaibhav Das Mundhra, Garena’s head of operations in India, told TechCrunch that Free Fire MAX would remain available alongside Free Fire India. While Free Fire India’s launch remained on hold, TechCrunch earlier reported that Garena continued developing games for the Indian market.
