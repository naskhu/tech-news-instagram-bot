Amazon introduces Kindle Click, a $35 remote for turning pages

The Kindle Click is a compact Bluetooth remote designed to let readers turn pages without touching their Kindle. The new accessory is compatible with all new Kindle devices, as well as Kindle models released in 2024 and later.
