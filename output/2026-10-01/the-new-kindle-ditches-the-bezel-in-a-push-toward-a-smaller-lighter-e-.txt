The new Kindle ditches the bezel in a push toward a smaller, lighter e-reader

The new Kindle starts at $149.99 and is available in Ube and Graphite. The Kindle Colorsoft, which Amazon introduced in 2024 as its first Kindle with a color display, also adopts the thinner, lighter, flush-front design used across the refreshed lineup.
