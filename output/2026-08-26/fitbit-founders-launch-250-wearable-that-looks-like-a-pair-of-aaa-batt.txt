Fitbit founders launch $250 wearable that looks like a pair of AAA batteries.

Featuring built-in GPS, continuous biometric tracking, and AI-powered voice logging, it lets wearers send their location and emergency alerts to contacts without a phone, and lasts for up to five days before needing a charge.
