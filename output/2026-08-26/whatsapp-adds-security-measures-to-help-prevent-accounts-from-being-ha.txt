WhatsApp Adds Security Measures to Help Prevent Accounts From Being Hacked - CNET

WhatsApp announced Tuesday that it has two new security features to help prevent accounts from being hacked — upgrading PINs to passwords and allowing users to add more passkeys. To create a password, go to your WhatsApp Settings, select Account, then Password.
