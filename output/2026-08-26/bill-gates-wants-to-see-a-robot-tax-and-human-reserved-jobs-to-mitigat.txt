Bill Gates wants to see a robot tax and 'Human Reserved' jobs to mitigate harms from AI | TechCrunch

A tax would slow the rush away from human labor a little and raise money for retraining and a stronger safety net. He also proposes setting aside certain jobs as “Human Reserved,” essentially barring AI from being used in certain tasks.
