Meta agrees to sweeping changes to restrict kids' access to its apps as part of settlement with states | TechCrunch

A Meta spokesperson told TechCrunch that while the company already mutes notifications for teens at night, the new setting will block them altogether after midnight. Meta said it will continue to prevent teens from following or interacting with accounts it considers age-inappropriate.
