Instagram’s ‘First Draft’ trims your Reels clips for you

The Reels-editing feature automatically cuts out pauses across your clips. But unlike Adobe’s feature, Instagram’s First Draft tool doesn’t use AI, according to Instagram spokesperson Nicole Rechtszaid.
