WhatsApp adds stronger two-step verification, multiple passkeys

WhatsApp has started rolling out several new account security features, including support for multiple passkeys and stronger two-step verification. According to WhatsApp, more than 3 billion people in over 180 countries use its messaging service to communicate with family and friends.
