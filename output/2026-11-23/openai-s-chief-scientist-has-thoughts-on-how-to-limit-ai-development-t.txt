OpenAI’s chief scientist has thoughts on how to limit AI development | TechCrunch

A new blog post from OpenAI’s chief scientist Jakub Pachocki discusses potential limits on AI development, with a focus on a measurement called perplexity. In Pachochki’s argument, benchmarks that focus on AI’s “downstream capabilities” have quickly become outdated as the technology advances.
