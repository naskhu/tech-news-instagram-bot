Apple tested a stylus for the foldable iPhone, but probably won’t ship one.

According to Mark Gurman , the company built a stubbier Apple Pencil that magnetically attached to the side of the phone for storage and charging. But it was awkward in certain positions and could leave marks on the delicate foldable screens.
