Psilocybin Might Make Your Brain Live in the Moment

Under the influence of psychedelics, the usual organization of the human brain loosens. The researchers tracked moment-by-moment changes across 332 regions of the brain using functional magnetic resonance imaging and electroencephalography, two methods of measuring brain activity.
