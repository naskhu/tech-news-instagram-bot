Titan OS raises $46 million to expand its smart TV OS internationally | TechCrunch

Barcelona-based smart TV operating system maker Titan OS has raised $46 million in new funding, consisting of both equity and debt, the company announced on Wednesday. The company now serves 30 million users, up from 18 million last year.
