Anthropic continues compute-gobbling streak in $45B deal with Nscale | TechCrunch

Anthropic has signed a deal to rent about $45 billion in AI compute from Nscale , a British AI infrastructure company, a source familiar with the deal told TechCrunch. In July, the company also signed a $5 billion compute-related deal with AMD.
