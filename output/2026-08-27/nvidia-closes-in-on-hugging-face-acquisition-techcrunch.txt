Nvidia closes in on Hugging Face acquisition | TechCrunch

Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, The Information reported Wednesday night, citing a source familiar with the matter. Owning Hugging Face would give Nvidia the ability to sell that unused capacity to Hugging Face’s customers.
