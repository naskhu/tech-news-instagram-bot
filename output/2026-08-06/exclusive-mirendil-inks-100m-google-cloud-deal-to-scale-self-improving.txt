Exclusive: Mirendil inks $100M+ Google Cloud deal to scale self-improving AI | TechCrunch

AI lab Mirendil has signed a multi-year partnership with Google Cloud to source compute capacity for its self-improving AI research, TechCrunch has exclusively learned. The deal is worth upwards of $100 million, Mirendil’s co-founder and CEO, Benham Neyshabur, told TechCrunch.
