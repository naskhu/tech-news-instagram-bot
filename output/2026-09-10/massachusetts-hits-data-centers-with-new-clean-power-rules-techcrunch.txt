Massachusetts hits data centers with new clean power rules | TechCrunch

A new state mandate would require developers building data centers larger than 25 megawatts to provide clean power or pay into a ratepayer protection fund. In July, New York’s governor stopped construction of new data centers 50 megawatts or larger.
