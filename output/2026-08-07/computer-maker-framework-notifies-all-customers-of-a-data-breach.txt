Computer maker Framework notifies ‘all customers’ of a data breach

Framework’s spokesperson Eric Schumacher told TechCrunch that the breach affected “all customers,” but declined to specify a specific number. In its email to customers, Framework also included the email Metabase sent to the company, which says hackers accessed Framework’s cloud instance.
