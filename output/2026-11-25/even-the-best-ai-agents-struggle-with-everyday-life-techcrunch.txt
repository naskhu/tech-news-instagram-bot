Even the best AI agents struggle with everyday life | TechCrunch

According to ShellBench, a new benchmark for everyday agent work, even the most advanced models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta failed more than a third of the time. TechCrunch has reached out to Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic, and the researchers in the study.
