X’s latest update for creators focuses on vertical video

X announced new creation tools Wednesday, many of them focused on short-form vertical video. These include the “ Video Kickstart Bonus, ” an invitation-only pilot that rewards selected creators for publishing original vertical videos under three minutes long and without watermarks.
