Waymo taps Toyota to supply robotaxis for Japan and the U.K. | TechCrunch

Waymo will begin testing autonomous Toyota bZ4X vehicles in Japan next year as part of a broader partnership with the automaker to provide electric vehicles for robotaxi operations in international markets, including the U.K.
