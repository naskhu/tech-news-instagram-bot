DoorDash said it’s only received ‘several hundred’ orders from AI agents

DoorDash has its own agents, including a text-to-order agent it launched in September , a CLI agent introduced in July , and a chatbot called Ask DoorDash it launched it June, which allows users to place orders through prompts and photos .
