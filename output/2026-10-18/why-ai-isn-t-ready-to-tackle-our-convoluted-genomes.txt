Why AI Isn’t Ready to Tackle Our Convoluted Genomes

Transcription gets started by proteins called transcription factors, which are like the operations managers of gene regulation. When they interact with the genome, transcription factors bind to pieces of DNA called enhancers—which present a puzzle of their own.
