Anthropic is committing 5% of its shares to a ‘Public Benefit Fund’

The new Anthropic Public Benefit Fund will be a wholly owned LLC, which the company says will give it “flexibility.” It offered little information about how it would use the money, other than saying it wouldn’t fund lobbying or political donations.
