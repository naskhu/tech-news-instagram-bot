Nvidia launches new open models to run agents in the enterprise and on PCs | TechCrunch

Nvidia on Thursday launched new open-weight models designed for always-on agents and edge computing. Of the two Bolt models, Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB is a 74-billion parameter model designed for local agentic tasks.
