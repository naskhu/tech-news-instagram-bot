Cities want to put the boot on robotaxis

Frustrated by federal gridlock, cities and states across the political spectrum are taking matters into their own hands to rein in autonomous vehicles. Minneapolis is considering an ordinance to require human monitors inside autonomous vehicles, which would effectively ban driverless operations.
