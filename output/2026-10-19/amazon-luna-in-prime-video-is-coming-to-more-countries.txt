Amazon Luna in Prime Video is coming to more countries.

Amazon started letting US and UK users stream games from Luna inside Prime Video in July , and today the feature will be available in Canada, France, Italy, and Spain to Amazon Prime subscribers on Fire TVs, Amazon says .
