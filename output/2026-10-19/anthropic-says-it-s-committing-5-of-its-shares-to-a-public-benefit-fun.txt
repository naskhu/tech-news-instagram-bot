Anthropic says it’s committing 5% of its shares to a ‘public benefit fund’ | TechCrunch

Anthropic said that it will commit 5% of its shares to the fund — structured as a wholly-owned entity within the company — with the option to contribute an additional 2% for every 50% increase in Anthropic’s value.
