California's proposed 'billionaire tax' fails to pass | TechCrunch

California voters have rejected Proposition 40, the state’s proposed one-time tax on billionaires, the Associated Press reported Monday . But opponents argued that it would scare California’s wealthiest residents out of the state and ultimately disincentivize growth.
