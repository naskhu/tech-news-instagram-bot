Physical AI chip developer SiMa.ai hits $1.45B valuation | TechCrunch

SiMa.ai , a startup developing chips and software that allow robots, drones, cameras, and other devices to run AI directly on the device, has raised a $150 million Series C at a $1.45 billion valuation.
