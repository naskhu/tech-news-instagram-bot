Some Pancreatic Cells Are Just One Genetic Tweak Away From Treating Diabetes

The researchers engineered ductal cells, which—as some previous studies had found—occasionally transform into beta cells all on their own. Through this process, the researchers found that taking out a gene called ALDH3B2 turned ductal cells into beta-like cells at a higher rate.
