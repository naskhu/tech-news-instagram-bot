The iPhone Duo may already have its first killer app: a virtual Walkman

Indie developer Vidit Bhargava has been showing off an app for the first Apple foldable that replicates the experience of using a classic Walkman. Open the Duo to pick and 'insert' the cassette, Close the Duo to start listening.
