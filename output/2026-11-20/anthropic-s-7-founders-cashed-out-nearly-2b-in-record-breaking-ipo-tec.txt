Anthropic's 7 founders cashed out nearly $2B in record-breaking IPO | TechCrunch

Anthropic became a public company on Thursday, raising a total of $81.3 billion, including $1.95 billion for its seven founders. Just for comparison: When Facebook had its then record-breaking IPO in 2012, it raised $16 billion on IPO day.
