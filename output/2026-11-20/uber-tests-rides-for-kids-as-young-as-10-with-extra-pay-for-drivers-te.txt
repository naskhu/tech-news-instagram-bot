Uber tests rides for kids as young as 10, with extra pay for drivers | TechCrunch

The Uber Kids program is stacked with mandatory requirements for parents and drivers. Eligible drivers will earn $7 more on every Uber Kids trip compared to a standard one, the company says on the dedicated Uber Kids web page.
