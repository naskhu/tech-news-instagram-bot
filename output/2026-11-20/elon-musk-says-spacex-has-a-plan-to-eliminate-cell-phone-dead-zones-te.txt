Elon Musk says SpaceX has a plan to eliminate cell phone dead zones | TechCrunch

CEO Elon Musk said SpaceX has completed the design of a miniature rooftop cell tower that could provide better coverage to mobile phones and eliminate dead zones inside buildings where signals from its Starlink satellites have trouble reaching.
